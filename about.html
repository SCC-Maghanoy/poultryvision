About the project

Technology with flock care in mind.

PoultryVision is a student project exploring how chicken behavior monitoring could help farmers recognize changes in their flock and decide when closer observation may be needed.

See how it works

Our direction

Make observation more organized.

The project aims to bring behavior observations into one accessible workflow, while keeping the farmer's judgment and animal-care expertise central.

Practical

Keep the experience understandable for people who care for chickens in everyday farm settings.

Responsible

Treat possible behavior flags as information to review, not as proof that a chicken has a specific disease.

In development

The website is an introduction. The scanning application and its analysis workflow are still being planned.
